# Presentation, Demonstration Video & Submission Plan

## 1. Submission Overview & Deliverables Checklist

To maximize score under the **Presentation** and **Impact** judging dimensions, OUTPOST: MARS provides a polished, cohesive submission package:

- [ ] **Project Title:** OUTPOST: MARS
- [ ] **Tagline:** A browser-based 2D Mars outpost mission trainer empowering kids (ages 7–12) to survive the Red Planet using authentic NASA space weather and life-support science.
- [ ] **Live Interactive Web Demo:** Hosted on GitHub Pages / Vercel Free Tier (100% free, zero installation, Chromebook-ready, zero live API dependency).
- [ ] **Open-Source Repository:** Public GitHub repository with clean commit history, architectural documentation, and MIT License.
- [ ] **NASA Data Resource Citations:** Comprehensive bibliography citing NASA DONKI space weather events, MSL Curiosity RAD Gale Crater dosimetry ($0.64 \pm 0.12\text{ mSv/day}$), Mars 2020 Perseverance MEDA weather, NASA BVAD life support standards, and CSA space agriculture research.
- [ ] **2-Minute Video Pitch:** Clear, engaging walkthrough video with audio narration and captions.
- [ ] **7-Slide Presentation Deck:** Executive overview in PDF/slides format covering problem, solution, science, technology, accessibility, and educational impact.

## 2. 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> *"Sending humans to Mars is humanity's next giant leap—but Mars is an unforgiving world with unbreathable air, subzero cold, and lethal space weather. How do we prepare the next generation to explore it?*  
> *Meet **OUTPOST: MARS**, a free, browser-based 2D mission trainer designed for kids aged 7 to 12. As Outpost Commander at Jezero Crater, young explorers balance three vital resources—Power, Life Support, and Storm Protection—to keep their crew safe against real space weather events derived from NASA’s DONKI database and Curiosity RAD dosimetry. Guided by friendly advisor Commander Aiko, kids discover that with real NASA science, even the harshest world can become a thriving home."*

## 4. 7-Slide Presentation Deck Outline

1. **Slide 1: Title & Vision** — Project name, hero screenshot, solo credits, mission statement.
2. **Slide 2: The Challenge & Audience** — The gap in accessible space STEM education for children aged 7–12; why hands-on simulation beats passive textbooks.
3. **Slide 3: The Solution (OUTPOST: MARS)** — 2D mission trainer overview, 3-resource loop (Power, Life Support, Storm Protection), 4 essential modules, Commander Aiko pedagogy.
4. **Slide 4: NASA & Space Agency Data Integration** — Grounding game mechanics in DONKI space weather events, MSL Curiosity RAD dosimetry baselines ($0.64 \pm 0.12\text{ mSv/day}$), Perseverance MEDA weather, and CSA space agriculture.
5. **Slide 5: Architecture & Technology** — Zero-cost static web architecture, Vite + TypeScript + HTML5 Canvas, bundled offline snapshot data, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
6. **Slide 6: Educational Impact & Evaluation** — Multidisciplinary 100-point rubric results, student engagement feedback, STEM learning outcomes.
7. **Slide 7: Roadmap & Next Steps** — Multi-outpost networks, classroom collaborative mode, live demo link, and open-source invitation.
