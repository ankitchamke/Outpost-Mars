# OUTPOST: MARS

## What This Is

OUTPOST: MARS is a browser-based 2D Mars outpost mission trainer designed for children aged 7–12. Players take on the role of Outpost Commander, managing power, life support, and storm protection on the Martian surface while responding to space weather events and astronaut health challenges grounded in authentic NASA, OSDR, RadLab, and CSA datasets.

## Core Value

Translate real NASA and space agency data into an intuitive, empowering, and scientifically grounded 2D mission trainer where young learners discover how authentic science and engineering keep humans alive on Mars.

## Target Audience & Pedagogy

- **Primary Audience:** Children aged 7–12 (Upper Elementary / Middle School STEM).
- **Tone:** Encouraging, scientifically authentic, and adventurous without being punishing or overly complex.
- **Pedagogical Loop:** Learn a real space concept → Make an engineering decision → Observe consequences → Receive positive reinforcement & transparent scientific debrief.

## Gameplay Model

### 3 Core Managed Resources
1. **Power:** Kilowatts (kW) generated by solar panels and stored in battery cells to run systems through the Martian night and extreme subzero cold.
2. **Life Support:** Combined consumable reserve sustaining the crew (with Oxygen, Water, and Nutritional sub-values visible as educational breakdowns).
3. **Storm Protection:** Structural and radiation shielding readiness (regolith berms, shelter integrity) protecting the crew from space radiation.

### Crew Safety (Outcome Indicator)
- **Crew Safety** is an **outcome indicator** (Nominal / Caution / Danger), NOT a spendable resource. It reflects the overall health, radiation exposure, and life-support stability of the 4 astronauts.

### 4 Essential MVP Modules
1. **Habitat:** Living quarters, communications hub, and crew exercise/health station (counteracting physical deconditioning).
2. **Solar Array & Battery:** Consolidated photovoltaic generation and overnight energy storage unit.
3. **Life Support System:** Consolidated atmospheric oxygen extraction, water recycling, and filtration unit.
4. **Storm Shelter / Shielding:** Compacted Martian regolith and radiation barrier.
*(Optional Upgrade / Scenario Event: CSA Hydroponics Greenhouse).*

## Scientific Foundations Summary

- **Radiation Baseline `[REAL]`:** Uses MSL Curiosity RAD Gale Crater baseline of $0.64 \pm 0.12\text{ mSv/day}$ (first 300 sols) as a representative Martian surface figure.
- **Solar Storm Scenario `[GAME-SIMPLIFIED]`:** The $+50\text{ mSv}$ storm surge is an **intentionally exaggerated hypothetical game scenario** (1,000x larger than the $\approx 50\ \mu\text{Sv}$ event observed on Mars in 2013), designed to create clear gameplay stakes; the debrief explicitly explains this distinction.
- **Regolith Attenuation `[GAME-SIMPLIFIED]`:** The $80\%$ reduction is a hypothetical game-balance parameter; real physics depends on composition, depth, and secondary neutrons.
- **Meteorology `[REAL]`:** Grounded in Perseverance MEDA observations at Jezero Crater (representative 700–800 Pa atmospheric pressure, extreme subzero daily temperature swings).
- **Life Support `[REAL]`:** Derived from NASA BVAD ($0.84\text{ kg } O_2/\text{day}$, $2.0\text{--}2.5\text{ L/day}$ direct water intake, $\sim 2,700\text{--}3,000\text{ kcal/day}$).
- **Bone Loss `[REAL in 0g]`:** $1.0\%\text{--}1.5\%/\text{month}$ loss in weight-bearing bones from orbital human studies; partial 0.38g gravity effect on humans is noted as scientifically unverified.
